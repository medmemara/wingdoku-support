Progress and accounts

  • Start as a guest. Before changing devices, link Apple or Google in Settings → Progress & account.
  • Use the same linked account on another device to restore synced progress. Check the save status before removing the app.
  • Exported files are personal copies. Use your linked account to restore online progress.
  • You can continue an already started puzzle offline. New puzzles need an internet connection. Pending moves and item refills are kept on the device and sent when you reconnect.
  • Keep all your devices on the latest app version when using new puzzle or item rules.

Items, lives and ads

  • Unused radar, hint and scanner items carry over to the next puzzle and restarts.
  • When an item reaches zero, finish a rewarded video to refill three of that item. An unavailable ad also refills three; closing early gives no reward.
  • At zero lives, completing a rewarded ad or an unavailable ad restores three lives and continues the same puzzle.
  • The same refill is not granted again when a saved request is resent.

Delete your account

Use Settings → Progress & account → Delete account and progress. If you cannot use the app, you can request deletion by email.
